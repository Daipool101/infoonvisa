## Disagreements from this run

### united-kingdom-to-australia — live says `evisa`, research says `eta` (high confidence, 98 searches)

**Why:** An ordinary United Kingdom passport holder travelling to Australia for tourism requires either an Electronic Travel Authority (ETA) (subclass 601) or an eVisitor (subclass 651), both of which are electronic travel authorisations linked to the passport.

**Could not confirm:** none

**Pages opened directly:** https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration, https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/

**Found by search:** Visas and immigration - GOV.UK, Immigration and citizenship, australia.com, smartraveller.gov.au, doineedavisa.uk, ternvisa.com, mytravelready.ai, migrationrepublic.com.au, revolut.com

### india-to-indonesia — live says `evisa`, research says `voa` (high confidence, 68 searches)

**Why:** Citizens of India holding an ordinary Indian passport are eligible for Visa on Arrival (VoA) or Electronic Visa on Arrival (e-VOA) for tourism purposes in Indonesia, allowing a stay of up to 30 days, extendable once for another 30 days.

**Could not confirm:** none

**Pages opened directly:** https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/, https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/

**Found by search:** The Official eVisa website for Indonesia, vfsevisa.id, cgibali.gov.in, indonesia.nl

### india-to-qatar — live says `voa`, research says `visa_free` (high confidence, 57 searches)

**Why:** Indian citizens holding an ordinary passport can obtain a free multiple-entry tourist visa on arrival in Qatar for a stay of up to 30 days, extendable for another 30 days, provided they meet specific conditions.

**Could not confirm:** none

**Pages opened directly:** https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/

### india-to-south-africa — live says `evisa`, research says `eta` (high confidence, 47 searches)

**Why:** Indian citizens require an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) for tourism to South Africa, which is applied for online and is valid for up to 90 days.

**Could not confirm:** The exact launch date of the ETA system (some sources say November 2025, others August 2026) and whether the older eVisa system is still a viable alternative for Indian citizens or if the ETA has fully superseded it for tourism purposes.

**Pages opened directly:** https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/

**Found by search:** visahacks.in, godigit.com, radvisionworld.com, passportsandvisas.com, y-axis.com, hindustantimes.com

### united-states-to-indonesia — live says `voa`, research says `evisa` (high confidence, 26 searches)

**Why:** US citizens traveling to Indonesia for tourism can obtain an Electronic Visa on Arrival (e-VOA) online or a Visa on Arrival (VoA) upon arrival, both allowing a 30-day stay extendable once for another 30 days.
